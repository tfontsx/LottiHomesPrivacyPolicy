2. What the app is for

The app has two sides. Clients use it to follow their own build or renovation: to see their stages and dates, photos and documents we choose to share, and to message their project manager. Our team uses the admin side to manage leads, projects, contractors and conversations. The app is used only for running that service. It is not a marketing or advertising app.

3. Information we collect

We collect only what the service needs.

Information you give us or that we record when we set you up as a client

  • Your name, email address, mobile number and postal address.
  • A second contact's name and phone number, if you ask us to add one.
  • How you prefer to be contacted (SMS, email or call).
  • Your project's site address, type, stages, dates, status and notes that we choose to share with you.

Information you create in the app

  • Messages you send to us, and the photos or documents you attach to them.
  • Your notification choices (which kinds of alerts you want).
  • Your password. We never see or store it ourselves: our authentication provider keeps only a secure, salted hash of it.

Information that identifies your phone to our notification service

  • A device notification token and the identifier our notification provider (Google Firebase) assigns to the app on your phone, so that alerts such as a stage update or a reply reach you. The token is removed when you sign out.

Information that happens in the background

  • When your phone connects to our servers, our hosting provider records technical details such as your IP address, device type and the time, for security and to keep the service running.
  • The app downloads public weather information from the Bureau of Meteorology. No personal information is sent with that request, apart from the IP address any website receives.

Information for our staff (admin side only)

  • Our staff may choose to connect a Google Calendar so project bookings appear in it. If they do, Google gives the app a permission token for that calendar only.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone or camera recordings, browsing history, advertising identifier, health, financial or payment details, or any sensitive information.
  • We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

4. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • set up and run your account and show you your own project;
  • let you and your project manager message each other;
  • send you notifications you have not turned off, such as stage updates, weather notices and replies;
  • keep the app secure and prevent misuse;
  • answer your requests, including requests for a copy of your data; and
  • meet our legal, tax, insurance and warranty obligations.

We do not use your information for tracking, advertising or marketing, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to build profiles of you.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers who run the app for us, and only so they can provide that service:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database, sign-in system and file storage.
  • Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging), and Apple's and Google's own notification services, which deliver notifications to your phone.
  • Google Calendar, only if one of our staff connects it on the admin side.

These providers may store or process information inside or outside Australia, including in Sydney, Australia. We take reasonable steps to make sure they handle it securely and only for our purposes.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety or our legal rights.

Inside the app, each client can see only their own project. Photos and documents are hidden from you until we choose to share them.

6. Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint

If you choose to, you can use Face ID, Touch ID or your phone's fingerprint or passcode check to sign in. The check is carried out by your phone. The app never receives, sees or stores your face, fingerprint or any biometric data. It only learns whether the check passed.

To make this work, the app keeps your sign-in details in your phone's secure storage (the iOS Keychain or the Android keystore). They stay on your phone, are not backed up or synced, and are removed if you delete your account or the saved password stops working.

7. Your photos and files

The app asks for access to your photo library only when you tap to attach a photo, and only the photos you pick are read. If you tap Save on a photo we shared with you, the app asks permission to add it to your library. It never reads your other photos. Files are only read when you choose them.

8. Notifications

The app asks permission to send notifications. You can switch each kind on or off in Settings in the app, or turn them all off in your phone's settings.

9. How we keep it secure

  • Everything sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • Access rules in our database mean a client can read only their own project, messages and shared files.
  • Your sign-in session is kept in your phone's secure storage, and passwords are stored only as secure hashes by our authentication provider.
  • Our staff accounts are limited to the people who need them.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to cause you serious harm, we will tell you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as the law requires.

10. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account and while we are building for you. Our project records, including messages and documents, are then kept for 7 years to meet our legal, tax and warranty obligations. After that they are deleted or made anonymous. Device notification tokens are deleted when you sign out.

11. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your information. You can change your mobile number and contact preference in Settings in the app. To correct your name, email or address, message your project manager.
  • Ask for a copy. Email us at lottihomes@outlook.com or message your project manager in the app and ask for a copy of your information. We will email it to you.
  • Delete your account. In the app, go to Settings and choose Delete account. Your login is closed straight away and we are told. We keep your project records for the period in section 10, then delete them.
  • Make a complaint. Contact us at the address in section 1. If you are not happy with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

12. Children

The app is for our clients, who are adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.
